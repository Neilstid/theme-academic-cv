# 🚀 This Week in AI: Agentic Revolution, Multimodal Breakthroughs, and the Rise of 'Focal Models'

*Hold onto your hats, folks! This week in AI is like a sci-fi movie unfolding in real time. We’ve got models that outperform giants like GPT-5.5 at a fraction of the cost, AI agents taking over your desktop (with your permission, of course), and robots that are getting smarter by the day. If you’ve ever wondered what the future of AI looks like, it’s happening *right now*. Let’s break it down in a way that even your grandma would understand (no offense, Grandma).*

## **1. MiniMax-M3: The David That Outperforms Goliath (GPT-5.5) at 10% the Cost**

Imagine a tiny, super-efficient robot that can do the work of a giant, clunky machine—but faster, cheaper, and with fewer complaints. That’s **MiniMax-M3** for you! Released on June 1, 2026, this model is like the Swiss Army knife of AI: it handles **1 million tokens of context**, understands **text, images, and videos**, and can even **operate your desktop autonomously**. Oh, and it does all this for just **5-10% of the cost** of models like GPT-5.5. Yes, you read that right!

### **The Secret Sauce: MSA Architecture**

MiniMax-M3 uses something called **MiniMax Sparse Attention (MSA)**, which is a fancy way of saying it’s really good at focusing on what’s important. Traditional AI models get slower and more expensive as they handle longer texts, but MSA makes MiniMax-M3 **9 times faster** at processing 1 million tokens. It’s like having a supercomputer that sips energy like a Prius.

### **Benchmarks: Beating the Big Guys**

MiniMax-M3 isn’t just fast—it’s *smart*. It outperformed GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro on **SWE-Bench Pro**, a test for software engineering tasks, scoring **59.0%** compared to their mid-50s scores. It also aced **OSWorld-Verified**, a benchmark for computer control tasks, with a **70.06%** completion rate. In simpler terms, if AI models were students, MiniMax-M3 would be the valedictorian.

### **Real-World Magic: Autonomous Tasks**

MiniMax didn’t just talk the talk—they walked the walk. They tested M3 on three real-world tasks:

1. **Reproducing a Research Paper**: M3 read a complex AI research paper, planned the experiments, and executed them **autonomously for 12 hours**. It even generated figures and commits—no human intervention needed!
2. **Optimizing CUDA Kernels**: M3 took a non-functional code skeleton and optimized it to **9.4 times faster** on NVIDIA GPUs. Most models gave up after 30 tries, but M3 kept going like the Energizer Bunny.
3. **Training Models Autonomously**: M3 took four pre-trained models and ran the entire training cycle—**data synthesis, training, evaluation, and iteration**—without any human help.

### **Why This Matters**

MiniMax-M3 is a game-changer because it proves that **smaller, smarter models** can outperform giants without breaking the bank. It’s like discovering that your trusty bicycle can outrace a Ferrari—if the Ferrari cost 10 times more to fuel.

**🔗 Want to try it?** Check out the [MiniMax M3 API](https://minimax.ai) (technical report coming soon!).

## **2. Agentic AI Takes Over: OpenAI Codex and xAI’s grok-build**

Remember when AI was just a fancy chatbot? Those days are *long* gone. This week, **OpenAI Codex** started controlling Windows desktops via mobile, and **xAI’s grok-build** launched an autonomous coding agent for just **$1 per million tokens**. Meanwhile, Alibaba’s **Qwen-VLA** is teaching robots to do everything from sorting packages to dancing the tango (okay, maybe not the tango… yet).

### **OpenAI Codex: Your Desktop’s New Best Friend (or Boss?)**

OpenAI’s Codex is no longer just suggesting code—it’s **taking over your screen**. Now available on Windows, Codex can:
- **Click, type, and navigate apps** based on your commands (e.g., *“@Excel, generate a pivot table from this dataset”*).
- **Run remotely from your phone** while you relax on the couch.
- **Debug UI issues** by reproducing bugs automatically.

**Limitations?** It runs in the foreground, so it’ll take over your screen while working. Also, your screen data is processed by OpenAI’s servers, so maybe don’t use it for top-secret projects.

**📥 Ready to try?** Grab it from the [Microsoft Store](https://apps.microsoft.com) and update your ChatGPT mobile app.

### **xAI grok-build: The Coding Partner That Never Sleeps**

xAI’s **grok-build-0.1** is like having a coding buddy who never needs coffee breaks. For just **$1 per million input tokens**, it can:
- **Plan, write, and refactor code** across multiple steps.
- **Accept text AND images**, so you can feed it UI mockups or error screenshots.
- **Keep iterating** until the task is done—no hand-holding required.

**Benchmark Highlight**: Most models stop improving after a few tries, but grok-build keeps going like a marathon runner.

**🔗 Want to test it?** Head to the [xAI API](https://x.ai) or [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai).

### **Alibaba Qwen-VLA: One Model to Rule Them All (Robots, That Is)**

While Codex and grok-build focus on software, Alibaba’s **Qwen-VLA** is all about **robotics**. This single model can control **11 different types of robots**, from warehouse arms to drones. It’s like having a universal remote for robots—except way cooler.

**Why It Matters**: Qwen-VLA shows that **generalist AI models** can replace dozens of specialized controllers, making robotics more accessible and efficient.

## **3. JetBrains Mellum2: The Speedy ‘Focal Model’ for Software Engineering**

If MiniMax-M3 is the valedictorian, **JetBrains Mellum2** is the star athlete—fast, efficient, and built for teamwork. This **12B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) model** is designed for **low-latency tasks** like coding, debugging, and tool use. It’s not here to replace giant models; it’s here to **make them faster and cheaper**.

### **MoE Architecture: Small but Mighty**

Mellum2 uses a **Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) architecture**, which means it only activates **2.5B of its 12B parameters** at a time. This makes it **2 times faster** than comparable models while keeping performance competitive. Think of it as a sports car that only uses the fuel it needs.

### **Benchmarks: Speed Meets Performance**

Mellum2 holds its own against bigger models like Qwen3.5 and OLMo-3:
- **EvalPlus (78.4)**: Outperforms Qwen3.5 (4B) and OLMo-3 (7B).
- **BFCL v3 (66.3)**: Competitive with Qwen3.5 (9B) on tool use.
- **LiveCodeBench (37.2)**: Trails Qwen3.5 (9B) but leads OLMo-3 (7B).

### **Use Cases: Where Mellum2 Shines**

JetBrains positions Mellum2 as a **‘focal model’** for specific tasks in larger AI systems:
1. **Routing & Orchestration**: Decide which model or tool to use for a given task.
2. **Low-Latency RAG Pipelines**: Summarize retrieved context quickly.
3. **Sub-Agents**: Handle repetitive tasks like code linting or test generation.
4. **Private Deployments**: Self-hostable and commercial-friendly (thanks to the **Apache 2.0 license**).

### **Strengths & Limitations**

| **Strengths**                          | **Limitations**                          |
|----------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| ✅ **2× faster inference**             | ❌ **No multimodality** (text/code only) |
| ✅ **131K context window**             | ❌ **Trails in some coding benchmarks**  |
| ✅ **Apache 2.0 license**              | ❌ **Not for frontier tasks**           |

**🔗 Ready to dive in?** Check out [Mellum2 on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/collections/JetBrains/mellum-2) or the [technical report](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.31268).
